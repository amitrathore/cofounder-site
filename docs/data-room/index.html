Fundraising · Starter kit

A starter data room for your next financing.

Organize what investors are funding, who owns it, what obligations exist, and how the financing changes ownership.

Download Data Room Starter ZIPSee what’s included

Version 1.0 · Updated September 19, 2026 · 10 folders · 72 checklist items · CC BY 4.0

Start with the records you actually have

For a U.S. startup raising a SAFE, convertible note, or priced equity round. The default is a Delaware C corporation, with founder holding-LLC and existing-business transfer records where applicable. A pre-revenue NewCo can mark genuinely absent items “None” or “Not applicable.” Unknown is a different status.

The ZIP contains editable Markdown instructions, a document index, and folders. It contains no company evidence or completed financing agreements. Markdown files are plain text and can be edited in a text editor or copied into your preferred document tool.

Set up your room

  1. Download and unzip. Keep a clean copy of the starter package.
  2. Copy the folders into your company’s chosen private storage platform.
  3. Assign a room owner and complete company details in folder 00.
  4. Work through the index. Assign document owners, status, dates, and links to authoritative copies.
  5. Add your records. Separate signed documents from drafts; give confirmed “None” and “Not applicable” entries an explanation.
  6. Reconcile the evidence. Ownership, financials, IP rights, and financing assumptions should agree.
  7. Configure and test access before sharing. Folder names do not enforce permissions.

09 · Current financing and closing

Use the checklist for the selected instrument, plus common closing records. Preserve prior financing instruments in the cap-table index; link to authoritative copies instead of maintaining inconsistent duplicates.

  • SAFE
  • Convertible note
  • Priced round
  • Common closing

A standard YC SAFE is not a debt note and has no interest or maturity date. Use the actual instrument terms when modeling conversion. YC forms and guidance.

The priced-round categories reflect the NVCA model-document suite; counsel determines the transaction-specific package.

Keep wire instructions in a separately verified closing process, not in the generally shared data room. Never treat receipt of an emailed instruction change as verification.

SAFE

  • Proposed SAFE form, exact terms and deviations from the selected standard.
  • Side letters, including any pro rata, information or MFN rights.
  • Required corporate and founder approvals.
  • Aggregate SAFE schedule: investor, amount, execution/funding status, form/version and terms.
  • Dilution/conversion scenarios coordinated with all existing instruments.

Maintain separate drafts/, executed/, and superseded/ subfolders. Label draft status explicitly.

Convertible note

  • Note and any purchase agreement, with proposed terms.
  • Interest, maturity, conversion triggers, repayment and default terms.
  • Security, guarantees or subordination documents, if applicable.
  • Principal and accrued-interest schedule and conversion model.
  • Required approvals, waivers and investor side letters.

Maintain separate drafts/, executed/, and superseded/ subfolders. Label draft status explicitly.

Priced equity round

  • Term sheet and proposed amended charter.
  • Stock purchase agreement and disclosure schedules.
  • Investors’ rights, voting, and right-of-first-refusal/co-sale agreements as applicable.
  • SAFE/note conversion calculations, pool changes and post-closing cap table.
  • Closing approvals, required waivers and other transaction-specific documents.

Maintain separate drafts/, executed/, and superseded/ subfolders. Label draft status explicitly.

Common closing records

  • Closing checklist with responsible person, prerequisite and completion evidence.
  • Executed agreements and amendments, with all signature pages.
  • Funding confirmations reconciled to signed amounts; restrict bank information.
  • Updated ownership/instrument records and applicable filing receipts.
  • Post-closing obligations, responsible person and deadlines.

Maintain separate drafts/, executed/, and superseded/ subfolders. Label draft status explicitly.

Share in stages

Initial review

Deck, team, demo, summarized traction, financing ask and operating plan.

Serious diligence

Corporate records, capitalization, IP and technology summaries, commercial evidence and detailed financials.

Restricted diligence and closing

Sensitive people and tax records, unredacted contracts, and investor-specific closing documents.

Use named-user access and test permissions. Keep credentials, raw customer personal data, and privileged advice out of the general room. Share wire instructions through a separately verified closing process.

Readiness checklist

Complete against actual evidence. This template starts unpopulated; an unchecked item is not a finding of noncompliance.

  • Stock ledger, cap table, signed awards/instruments, reserves and approvals reconcile.
  • Outstanding shares and fully diluted assumptions are distinguished.
  • Founder holding-vehicle ownership, authority and relevant consents are documented, if applicable.
  • All material assets have documented ownership or adequate usage rights.
  • Existing-business asset transfers/licenses and required consents are recorded, if applicable.
  • Deck, financial model, books and commercial claims reconcile for the same stated periods.
  • Forecasts and predecessor figures are clearly identified.
  • Pipeline, LOIs, pilots, signed commitments and recognized revenue are distinguished.
  • Founder rights, financing approval requirements and relevant contract restrictions are identified.
  • Conversion/dilution scenarios include all instruments, side letters, accrued interest where applicable, and proposed pool changes.
  • Missing items and unknowns have an owner, impact assessment and resolution date.
  • None / Not applicable entries have a confirmed explanation.
  • Signed records include complete signatures and amendments; drafts are unmistakable.
  • Necessary sensitive records are restricted; credentials and privileged advice are excluded from general access.
  • Access is tested with representative Initial, Diligence and Restricted users; each can see only intended records.
  • Closing checklist tracks funding, approvals, executed documents, applicable filings and post-closing tasks.

Scenario checks: a pre-revenue NewCo should not invent historical revenue; a founder LLC needs look-through records; an asset-transfer NewCo needs a documented rights chain; a SAFE raise needs the selected form and all side letters; a note raise needs interest/maturity and conversion modeling; a priced round needs a reconciled conversion and closing package.

Founder structure and financing

If founders use a holding LLC, include its ownership, vesting, authority and consent records alongside the corporation’s documents. For a NewCo receiving existing assets, document ownership, transfers or licenses, approvals and assumed obligations.

The Cofounder Agreement v2 public review draft offers one possible starting point. Using it is not a financing requirement.

For transaction documents, consult YC’s SAFE forms and guidance and the NVCA priced-round model documents. A standard YC SAFE is not a debt note and has no interest or maturity date.
