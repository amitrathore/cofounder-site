# Access and maintenance

These labels are a sharing policy; this local folder template does not enforce permissions. Configure named-user access in the chosen data-room platform and test it before sharing.

| Level | Intended materials | Recipient stage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initial | Deck, team, demo, summary traction, financing ask and summarized plan | Initial investor review |
| Diligence | Corporate, capitalization, technical/IP summaries and detailed financial records | Serious investor diligence |
| Restricted | Tax/election records, sensitive people information, unredacted contracts and investor-specific closing materials | Individually authorized reviewers and counsel |

- Give each recipient only approved access. Avoid public links; revoke access when no longer needed.
- Redact unnecessary personal and banking identifiers; summaries can precede underlying records.
- Check contractual confidentiality restrictions before sharing contracts or customer references.
- Exclude credentials, raw customer personal data and privileged legal advice from the general room. Counsel manages privilege and limited disclosures separately.
- Keep investor-specific terms/negotiations restricted to their intended recipients.
- Keep drafts, signed records and superseded copies distinct. Set an as-of date on financials and cap tables.
- Update after material developments and each closing; log changes and notify affected reviewers when appropriate.
- Keep wire instructions in a separately verified closing process.
