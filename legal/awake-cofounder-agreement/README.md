# Awake Cofounder Agreement

This directory is the publication home for the **Awake Cofounder Agreement**, an open legal template for a specific two-tier founder structure: a California limited liability company used as the cofounders' governance and holding vehicle, together with a Delaware corporation used as the operating and fundraising company.

It is not a menu of interchangeable California LLC and Delaware corporation clauses, and it is not a substitute for the complete formation, equity-issuance, employment, intellectual-property, securities, tax, or governance package the venture may require.

## Source of truth

`awake-cofounder-agreement.md` is the canonical source. The Word, PDF, and online-reading versions must identify the same version and be reconciled with it before a release. If two formats conflict, do not guess: open an issue and have qualified counsel review the discrepancy.

## Before signing

Complete every required field, choose every option, remove all drafting notes, reconcile the schedules, and obtain qualified legal and tax advice. The presence of a downloadable file does not mean that the agreement is complete, current, suitable, or legally effective for a particular venture.
