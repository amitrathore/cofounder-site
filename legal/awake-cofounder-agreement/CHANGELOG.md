# Changelog

All notable publication and substantive changes to the Awake Cofounder Agreement are recorded here. Releases use semantic-style versioning: major versions may change the structure or legal posture, minor versions may change operative language, and patch versions are limited to corrections that do not intentionally change legal effect.

## 1.0 — 2026-08-02

- Established `awake-cofounder-agreement.md` as the canonical editable source.
- Selected Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International for the template text and removed the unresolved license placeholder.
- Separated the license from the legal and professional-services disclaimer.
- Added version, publication date, jurisdictional posture, and legal-review status.
- Clarified that the template describes one combined California-LLC/Delaware-corporation holding structure, not two alternative entity pathways.
- Added an adaptation and attribution protocol.
- Added a completion and drafting guide, formation-package overview, and contribution process.
- Published online, Word, source, and PDF access points.
- No independent legal review is represented by this release. Operative provisions remain subject to California and Delaware startup-counsel and tax review.
