# Changelog

## 1.0 — 2026-10-01

- Initial open template for individual advisory services covering technology and business strategy and commercial introductions.
- Fixed common share award with twelve quarterly vesting installments over three years, subject to approval and separate equity documents.
- Configurable share of net sales commissions for written Accepted Referrals, with accounting, protection period, and post-termination tail fields.
- Express exclusion of securities and financing transactions from referral commissions.
- Canonical Markdown, online reader, editable Word, PDF, completion guide, disclaimer, contribution instructions, and CC BY 4.0 license.
- Independent legal review: not recorded.
