# Awake Advisor Agreement

An open agreement for an individual advisor providing technology and business strategy advice and commercial introductions in exchange for a fixed common share award and participation in eligible sales commissions.

Run `python build.py` with `python-docx` to generate Word and HTML. Run `python build-pdf.py` with ReportLab to generate the PDF from the same Markdown. Render the Word and PDF editions to page images, inspect every page, and reconcile agreement text across Markdown, Word, online HTML, and PDF. Run the site link checks. Do not publish a stale PDF after source changes.

## Scope

This template is for an individual commercial advisor. It requires separate corporate approvals and equity documents to implement the share award. Its referral commissions are limited to lawful commercial sales, not investment or securities transactions. The parties must complete the award mechanics, rate and base, account attribution, protection period, post-termination tail, and jurisdiction-specific terms with qualified advisers.
