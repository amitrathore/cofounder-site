# Contributing

Submit corrections or improvements through the [repository issue tracker](https://github.com/amitrathore/cofounder-site/issues/new) or a pull request. Identify the version, section, proposed wording, and jurisdiction-specific assumptions. Do not submit signatures, private agreements, customer names, or confidential information.

The Markdown agreement is canonical. Rebuild and reconcile the HTML, Word, and PDF editions after changes; update the guide and changelog for substantive changes. Do not claim independent legal review without a documented review and scope. Template-text contributions may be distributed under this package's CC BY 4.0 license; code contributions follow the repository's MIT license.
