# Contributing

Corrections and improvements to the Awake Open Collaboration Agreement are welcome from founders, operators, investors, advisors, and qualified legal professionals.

Use the [cofounder-site issue tracker](https://github.com/amitrathore/cofounder-site/issues/new) to describe:

- the clause, page, or publication file involved;
- the issue or ambiguity;
- the proposed correction and reasoning;
- the relevant jurisdiction; and
- whether qualified counsel reviewed the proposal.

Substantive legal changes require documented review before inclusion in a numbered release. Because the PDF is authoritative for version 1.0, proposed text changes must not silently replace it; they belong in a later version with an updated changelog.
