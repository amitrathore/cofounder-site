# Awake Open Collaboration Agreement

The **Awake Open Collaboration Agreement** is an open legal framework for founders, operators, investors, advisors, and venture collaborators exploring projects, investments, introductions, commercial relationships, and ecosystem opportunities before forming a company or entering definitive project agreements.

## Before signing

This is an educational template, not legal advice. It must be reviewed and adapted by qualified counsel for the parties, facts, jurisdictions, and intended collaboration before use.
