One specific architecture

Not a choose-your-own-entity form.

This template assumes that a California LLC holds and governs the cofounders’ interests while owning securities of a Delaware corporation used for operations and fundraising. It is not a standalone California operating agreement or a standalone Delaware founders’ agreement. The structure can affect taxes, securities compliance, investor acceptance, fiduciary duties, and ownership records.
