# Open legal resources

This directory is the publication catalog for cofounder.community's open legal resources. Each resource has its own stable, descriptive directory containing its canonical source, editable and fixed-layout releases, license, disclaimer, drafting guidance, version history, and contribution instructions.

## Published resources

- [Awake Cofounder Agreement](./awake-cofounder-agreement/) — version 1.0; California LLC and Delaware corporation used together
- [Awake Open Collaboration Agreement](./awake-open-collaboration-agreement/) — version 1.0; California-oriented framework for exploring ventures and projects together
- [Awake Venture Memorandum of Understanding](./awake-venture-memorandum-of-understanding/) — version 1.0; entity-agnostic short-form statement of intent among venture partners, covering structure, equity, compensation, roles, and exit intent
- [Awake Territory Agreement](./awake-territory-agreement/) — version 1.0; general business territory operating agreement covering exclusivity, performance, income sharing, accounting, and termination
- [Awake Advisor Agreement](./awake-advisor-agreement/) — version 1.0; technology and business strategy services, quarterly common share vesting over three years, and commercial referral commissions

Each resource states its own license and review status. These materials are educational templates, not legal advice, and require review by qualified counsel before use.
