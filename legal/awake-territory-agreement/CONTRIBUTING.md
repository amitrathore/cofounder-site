# Contributing

Submit corrections or proposed improvements through the [repository issue tracker](https://github.com/amitrathore/cofounder-site/issues/new) or a pull request. Identify the version, section, problem, proposed wording, and any jurisdiction-specific assumptions. Do not submit private agreements, signatures, customer details, or confidential information.

The Markdown agreement is canonical. Changes must reconcile the online, Word, and PDF editions, update the guide where relevant, and describe material changes in CHANGELOG.md. Do not describe a change as legally reviewed without a documented review and its scope.

By submitting template-text contributions, you agree they may be distributed under this package's CC BY 4.0 license. Code contributions follow the repository's MIT license. Contributions do not imply professional advice or endorsement.
