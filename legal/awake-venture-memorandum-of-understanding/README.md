# Awake Venture Memorandum of Understanding

This directory is the publication home for the **Awake Venture Memorandum of Understanding**, a short-form open template for people who have decided to build a venture together and want their intentions recorded before definitive agreements exist.

It covers the venture structure, founding equity, compensation beyond equity, roles and responsibilities, intellectual property, and an explicit, dated exit intent. It is deliberately short, and brevity is the drafting discipline it asks for; see **Length** below for what the current release actually runs to.

It is **not** a founders' agreement, an operating agreement, an investor term sheet, or a substitute for the formation, equity-issuance, intellectual-property, employment, securities, and tax package a venture requires.

## Two editions

The **annotated template** carries the version block, licence, field legend, and `[COUNSEL: …]` prompts. It is the reference edition.

The **fill-in copy** is the same memorandum with all of that apparatus removed: no notes, no legend, just the operative clauses with every blank highlighted. It is published as Word and PDF, generated from a single source by `build-fill-in.py` so the two cannot drift apart. The drafting guidance that used to sit inside the document now lives on the [online reading page](./index.html) and in [`guide.md`](./guide.md), so that what the Partners sign carries no instructions.

## Length

The operative memorandum runs to two pages of the published PDF at readable type: page one carries the venture, structure, equity, compensation, roles, and intellectual property; page two carries exit intent, definitive agreements, the binding terms, and signatures. A completed copy — with bracketed fields replaced by real values — remains two pages. Fitting the document onto a single page requires either type below eight points or removing roughly a quarter of its substance. Treat "one-page" as the drafting discipline the template aims at rather than a claim about the current release.

## Source of truth

`awake-venture-memorandum-of-understanding.md` is the canonical source. Any future Word, PDF, or online-reading version must identify the same version and be reconciled with it before release. If two formats conflict, do not guess: open an issue and have qualified counsel review the discrepancy.

## Where it sits

This memorandum is the step between exploring a venture and forming one. It typically follows an [Awake Open Collaboration Agreement](../awake-open-collaboration-agreement/) and precedes a founders' agreement such as the [Awake Cofounder Agreement](../awake-cofounder-agreement/), which it is designed to feed into.

## Before signing

Complete every required field, make every selection, remove all drafting notes, confirm the equity and role tables against what the Partners actually intend, and obtain qualified legal and tax advice. Pay particular attention to Clause 9: it determines which parts of the document are enforceable. The presence of a downloadable file does not mean the memorandum is complete, current, suitable, or legally effective for a particular venture.
