# Awake Venture Memorandum of Understanding

This directory is the publication home for the **Awake Venture Memorandum of Understanding**, a one-page open template for people who have decided to build a venture together and want their intentions recorded before definitive agreements exist.

It covers the venture structure, founding equity, compensation beyond equity, roles and responsibilities, intellectual property, and an explicit, dated exit intent. It is deliberately short: the document is meant to fit on a single page once completed.

It is **not** a founders' agreement, an operating agreement, an investor term sheet, or a substitute for the formation, equity-issuance, intellectual-property, employment, securities, and tax package a venture requires.

## Source of truth

`awake-venture-memorandum-of-understanding.md` is the canonical source. Any future Word, PDF, or online-reading version must identify the same version and be reconciled with it before release. If two formats conflict, do not guess: open an issue and have qualified counsel review the discrepancy.

## Where it sits

This memorandum is the step between exploring a venture and forming one. It typically follows an [Awake Open Collaboration Agreement](../awake-open-collaboration-agreement/) and precedes a founders' agreement such as the [Awake Cofounder Agreement](../awake-cofounder-agreement/), which it is designed to feed into.

## Before signing

Complete every required field, make every selection, remove all drafting notes, confirm the equity and role tables against what the Partners actually intend, and obtain qualified legal and tax advice. Pay particular attention to Clause 9: it determines which parts of the document are enforceable. The presence of a downloadable file does not mean the memorandum is complete, current, suitable, or legally effective for a particular venture.
