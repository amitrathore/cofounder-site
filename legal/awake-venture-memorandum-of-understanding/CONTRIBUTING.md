# Contributing

Corrections and improvements from founders, operators, investors, accountants, and lawyers are welcome.

## How to propose a change

Open a GitHub issue or pull request against this repository. Describe:

1. the affected version, file, clause, and jurisdiction;
2. the problem and practical scenario in which it appears;
3. the proposed language or publication change;
4. whether the proposal is substantive, editorial, or formatting-only;
5. supporting primary authority or professional reasoning; and
6. any conflicts of interest or material affiliations.

Do not include client confidences, personal information, signatures, privileged material, or venture secrets.

## Keep it short

This template's usefulness depends on its length. The current release runs to two pages of operative text, and it should not grow. Proposals that add clauses should say what they would remove, or explain why the addition earns the space. Material that belongs in a founders' agreement, operating agreement, or investor term sheet should be proposed there instead.

## Review and release

Editorial and formatting fixes may be accepted after maintainer review. Changes that may affect legal rights, tax treatment, securities compliance, employment obligations, intellectual-property ownership, equity, vesting, governance, exit rights, transfer rights, dispute resolution, or enforceability should receive documented review from appropriately qualified counsel before inclusion in a numbered release.

Changes to Clause 9 deserve particular care: it determines which parts of the document bind, and a change there can alter the legal effect of the whole memorandum.

Acceptance does not mean that cofounder.community, AwakeVC, Amit Rathore, or any contributor endorses a proposal for use in a particular transaction. Contributors agree that accepted contributions to the template text are published under CC BY 4.0 and that they have the right to submit them.

## Release checks

Before a release:

- reconcile Markdown and any HTML, Word, or PDF text;
- search for unresolved drafting notes and placeholders;
- confirm the version and dates in every format;
- confirm the operative memorandum has not grown beyond two pages;
- record substantive changes in `CHANGELOG.md`;
- record the legal-review status accurately; and
- verify all links and rendered documents.
