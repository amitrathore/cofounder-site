# Changelog

All notable publication and substantive changes to the Awake Venture Memorandum of Understanding are recorded here. Releases use semantic-style versioning: major versions may change the structure or legal posture, minor versions may change operative language, and patch versions are limited to corrections that do not intentionally change legal effect.

## 1.0 — 2026-08-22

- Established `awake-venture-memorandum-of-understanding.md` as the canonical editable source.
- Published a one-page memorandum covering venture structure, founding equity, compensation beyond equity, roles and responsibilities, intellectual property, exit intent, and definitive-agreement conversion.
- Adopted a mostly non-binding posture: Clauses 1 through 8 record intent, and Clause 9 identifies the confidentiality, exclusivity, costs, no-partnership, governing-law, and whole-understanding terms intended to bind and survive.
- Made exit intent an express, dated section rather than an assumption, covering preferred outcome, target window, valuation posture, process triggers, approval threshold, drag-along and tag-along intent, the position if no exit occurs in the target window, and individual side deals.
- Kept the template entity-agnostic, with entity type, jurisdiction, and governing law as required fields.
- Adopted the shared field legend (`[REQUIRED: …]`, `[SELECT ONE: …]`, `[OPTIONAL: …]`, `[COUNSEL: …]`, `[DRAFTING NOTE: …]`) used across the Awake legal templates.
- Selected Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International for the template text.
- Added a completion and drafting guide, disclaimer, license scope, and contribution process.
- No fixed-layout or Word release was published for this version.
- No independent legal review is represented by this release. Operative provisions, and the binding posture in particular, remain subject to counsel and tax review.
